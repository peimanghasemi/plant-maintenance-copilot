# Kiln ID fan

## Normal operation
The induced draft fan runs at 2 to 3 mm/s. Motor current follows the kiln draft demand.

## Imbalance and build-up
Dust build-up on the impeller causes imbalance. The typical sign is a slow increase in vibration at running speed with stable bearing temperature. Bearing damage shows rising temperature as well.

## Recommended actions
- Vibration above 5 mm/s with stable temperature: plan impeller cleaning at the next kiln stop.
- Vibration and temperature rising together: inspect the bearings, an unplanned kiln stop costs far more than an early inspection.
- Never run the fan above 7 mm/s for more than one shift.
