# Cement mill: main drive and bearings

## Normal operation
Vibration on the main bearings is normally 2 to 4 mm/s. Bearing temperature follows the load and stays below 75 C. Motor current follows the feed rate.

## Rising vibration
A slow rise in vibration together with a rising bearing temperature usually points to bearing wear or loss of lubrication. Check the lubrication system first: oil flow, oil temperature and filter pressure. Then check the alignment of the girth gear and pinion.

## Recommended actions
- Vibration above 6 mm/s or bearing temperature above 80 C: reduce feed and plan an inspection within 48 hours.
- Vibration above 8 mm/s: stop the mill and inspect the bearings before restart.
- After a bearing change, record the new baseline during the first 72 hours of operation.

## Spare parts
Main bearing sets and pinion bearings have long lead times. Order them as soon as the remaining useful life drops below 250 hours.
