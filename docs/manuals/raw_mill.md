# Raw mill: rollers and gearbox

## Normal operation
Vibration is normally 2.5 to 4.5 mm/s and depends strongly on the feed moisture. Short vibration spikes during feed changes are normal and are not a sign of damage.

## Rising pressure drop
A rising pressure drop across the mill with stable feed indicates wear of the nozzle ring or a blocked separator. Inspect the nozzle ring at the next planned stop.

## Recommended actions
- Persistent vibration above 6 mm/s: check roller bearings and the hydraulic pressure of the grinding rollers.
- Gearbox oil temperature above 70 C: check the oil cooler and take an oil sample for particle analysis.
- Plan roller re-surfacing together with other work in the same stop to reduce downtime.
