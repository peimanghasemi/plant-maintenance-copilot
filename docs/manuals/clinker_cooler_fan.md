# Clinker cooler fans

## Normal operation
Cooler fans run at 2 to 3.5 mm/s. Bearing temperature depends on the ambient temperature and is higher in summer.

## Typical problems
Damaged inlet dampers and worn bearings are the most common causes of trouble. A rising motor current at constant damper position can indicate mechanical friction.

## Recommended actions
- Vibration above 6 mm/s: inspect bearings and the coupling.
- Motor current more than 15% above normal at the same damper position: check for friction and inspect the bearings.
- Keep one spare fan bearing set on site for each cooler.
